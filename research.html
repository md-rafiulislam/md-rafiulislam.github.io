Undergraduate Thesis

Title: Gasification Technology for Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) in Rajshahi City Corporation: A Comprehensive Feasibility Study[cite: 1, 4]

Institution: Department of Chemical & Food Process Engineering, Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)[cite: 1, 4]

Supervisor: Sajib Dey, Assistant Professor[cite: 1, 4]

Timeline: December 2023[cite: 1, 4]

Core Methodology & Simulation Highlights

  • Modeled and simulated a downdraft gasifier using Aspen Plus software to evaluate the technical feasibility of converting municipal solid waste into high-value syngas[cite: 1, 4].
  • Optimized operating parameters, achieving a peak syngas yield of 63.73% (w/w) at an equivalence ratio of 0.04 and a steam-to-air ratio of 0.35[cite: 1, 4].
  • Simulated detailed molar syngas composition: 43.56% H2, 32.63% CO, 4.44% CO2, 0.51% CH4, and 18.79% N2[cite: 1, 4].
