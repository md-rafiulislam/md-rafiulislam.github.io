Undergraduate Thesis

Title: Gasification Technology for Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) in Rajshahi City Corporation: A Comprehensive Feasibility Study[cite: 1, 4].

Institution: Department of Chemical & Food Process Engineering, Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)[cite: 1, 4].

Supervisor: Sajib Dey, Assistant Professor[cite: 1, 4].

Timeline: December 2023[cite: 1, 4].

Core Methodology & Simulation

  • Modeled and simulated a downdraft gasifier using Aspen Plus software to evaluate the technical feasibility of converting municipal solid waste (MSW) into high-value syngas[cite: 1, 4].
  • Optimized operating parameters to achieve a peak syngas yield of 63.73% ($w/w$) at an equivalence ratio of 0.04 and a steam-to-air ratio of 0.35[cite: 1, 4].
  • Simulated detailed molar syngas composition: 43.56% $\text{H}_2$, 32.63% $\text{CO}$, 4.44% $\text{CO}_2$, 0.51% $\text{CH}_4$, and 18.79% $\text{N}_2$[cite: 1, 4].
